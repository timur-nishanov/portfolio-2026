khm-5.mp3, khm-6.mp3 (parts)
  rubberduck, "80 CC0 creture SFX 2" - https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-creture-sfx-2
  Licence: CC0. Both fetched from github.com/novincode/atomcut-library
  (packs/opengameart-80-cc0-creature-sfx, packs/opengameart-80-cc0-creture-sfx-2).

  khm-2  hurt_05.ogg: a short closed "hmf", no pitch change (f0 ~120-130 Hz).
  khm-4  the burst of cough_02.ogg (the "kh"), then grunt_02.ogg slowed 0.72x
         (the hum, f0 ~130-150 Hz), with cough_02's tail as air under the hum.
  khm-5  the onset of breath_02.ogg (the "kh"), then the soft closed-mouth
         start of grunt_08.ogg (the hum, f0 ~113-122 Hz), breath_02 as air.
  khm-6  the last burst of cough_03.ogg (the "kh"), then the low closed-mouth
         start of grunt_07.ogg (the hum, f0 ~106-119 Hz).
  The khm-5/6 hums are re-pitched with PSOLA (Praat overlap-add), which keeps
  the voice's own formants, onto a gentle fall into a male speaking range.

Processing (Python/numpy/Praat + ffmpeg): trimmed, high/low-passed (the
punches rolled off above ~3 kHz into a dull knock; the hums low-passed at
1.3-1.9 kHz to close the mouth; the "kh" bursts high-passed at 0.9 kHz, and
for khm-5/6 also low-passed at 4.5-5 kHz, where a throat "kh" sits), the
hums shaped to die away like a spoken "m",
short fades, peak-normalised to -1 dBFS, mono 44.1 kHz MP3 64 kbps.
The playback level (peaks about -28..-14 dBFS) is set at runtime.
